## Mechanistic assessment

The deployment probes can add real information. The draft says the tool supplies “paired canonical/serialized inputs with known feature semantics” (Diagnostic mechanism, lines 60–64). Sixteen signed basis probes identify a signed permutation directly, whereas ordinary finite-sample discriminative refitting need not identify that correspondence. The fresh deployment diagnostic also supplies deployment-specific labeled evidence. This is a plausible information advantage; it is not a proof that a competent refit cannot infer a useful deployed classifier, nor that the advantage will exceed R4's threshold. The draft appropriately allows refitting on the latest raw batch. R4 would measure the entire audit package, including extra diagnostic evidence, correspondence and the fixed response, rather than correspondence alone.

Waiting can have a real tradeoff: independent schema migration gives a later audit newer information, and an audit occupying a fixed horizon displaces fitting work. No assigned winner families or hidden-utility adjustments appear in the stated law. However, the exact scheduling and coordinate handling are essential to that argument and are not yet determined. In particular, c1 training uses canonical dev1, so initial deployment mismatch alone does **not** corrupt the pre-c2 ordinary gradients. Any proposed recovery cost must follow from the actual post-c2 sequence, not from assuming those earlier gradients were in mismatched coordinates.

The task has a route to solving without verification on reached roots: a classifier fitted directly in the final raw schema can predict in that same schema without recovering the canonical map. For unreached roots with a nonidentity initial map, canonical-only dev1 does not generally identify deployed feature semantics through refitting alone. That is an honest branch limitation to disclose, not a reason to manufacture a better never-verify result. Competence and effectiveness remain unmeasured.

## BLOCKER B1 — Mixed-schema fitting and competent-control semantics are undefined

**Section and quote:** Fully specified pre-run law, lines 130 and 133: “dev1 canonical; dev2 serialized using the current map”; “Maintain pooled and latest-only alternatives.” Diagnostic mechanism, lines 73–74: the response “changes the decoder to the currently probed map and performs one bounded optimizer update using visible fitting rows only.”

**Problem:** The draft never defines how one candidate decoder is applied to the two training formats. Applying a newly learned deployment inverse to canonical dev1 would corrupt that batch. Pooling canonical dev1 with raw dev2 without a defined conversion also gives incompatible coordinates. A latest-only raw fit can solve deployment only if its weights and inference decoder use matching coordinates. Changing a decoder while keeping, rebasing or resetting the weights yields different interventions. Ordinary visible diagnostic selection is similarly ambiguous: canonical dev1 diagnostics cannot simply be scored through a deployment decoder and treated as current deployed accuracy. These choices determine both whether the control is competent and what R4 compares.

**Concrete pre-seal fix:** Write mathematical prediction, training and validation equations for canonical dev1, raw dev2, each audit and hidden deployment rows. Specify the decoder orientation, how batch provenance is represented, what conversion is available without a probe, how latest-only candidates are represented, and exactly what happens to weights when reconciliation changes the decoder. Define how pooled/latest/incumbent candidates are compared on visible diagnostics with different schemas, including diagnostic weights and tie order. Keep the control and the verified executor identical except for the declared audit information and debit. State whether careless's “first four visible feature positions” means raw positions or decoded canonical positions, and apply that rule to the response as well. Do not make these decisions in implementation after sealing.

## BLOCKER B2 — Timing order and work accounting do not define one intervention

**Section and quote:** Waiting information, lines 89–93: “One ordinary fit update uses one slot”; verification “uses two slots”; “The first four slots precede c2.” Lines 99–100 say c2 verification can “test the candidate trained on the new batch.” The law, line 133, says to “charge the same declared slots for the paired alternative computation.”

**Problem:** At the instant c2 arrives, the inherited candidate has not yet been trained on the new batch. Testing a dev2-trained candidate requires an explicit ordering: an ordinary dev2 update before the audit, or an update inside the audit before evaluation. The stated response instead evaluates the current candidate and a reconciled alternative. Those are different interventions. It is also unclear whether an “update” means one minibatch gradient or an epoch, how two ordinary alternatives are initialized/updated, and whether computing both costs one slot or two. Thus a two-slot audit and a one-slot ordinary action cannot yet be compared as declared work. With canonical pre-c2 fitting, the asserted late cost “recover from earlier fitting on mismatched pooled coordinates” is not established merely by the timeline.

**Concrete pre-seal fix:** Freeze a slot-by-slot table for prefix, now, reached-later, unreached-later and never. Specify the c2 event order, exact audit-start state, whether the audit response fits before or after scoring, and the state returned on rejection. Define a work unit, paired-alternative computation debit, minibatch cursor/remainder handling and whether evaluation overhead is included in each action cost. Give every branch a concrete fallback, including whether unreached-later makes zero calls. Identify exactly which gradients can use mismatched pooled coordinates and how the remaining deadline creates the recovery cost. Acknowledge that the cost is a constructed work-budget tradeoff unless real elapsed tool costs are separately measured. No parameter search is needed to resolve this.

## BLOCKER B3 — The supposedly complete sampling and paired-replay law is incomplete

**Section and quote:** Fully specified law, lines 122 and 126: SeedSequence entropy is “[20261008, partition_index, family_index, root_index, stream_index]”; rows are “independent draws within its family.” Budget section, lines 238–239: “RNG law, parameters, draw order, family/row manifests ... command ... are sealed before outcome code.”

**Problem:** Covariance and a separator do not specify a feature distribution: the row law and feature mean are absent. The RNG table does not assign partition/stream indices or explain how family-level teacher/covariance draws avoid changing with root_index. Root counts beyond realism, row ordering, signed-permutation draw procedure and shared versus repetition-specific optimizer streams are not fixed. The first paragraph promises one shared prefix, while repetitions differ in optimizer order and behaviours use different fit fractions; the intended prefix-sharing boundary is therefore unclear. The promised execution command and manifest construction are also absent. A faithful implementer would have to choose an experiment after the seal.

**Concrete pre-seal fix:** State the exact distribution, for example zero-mean multivariate Gaussian if that is intended, and its deterministic numerical sampling convention. Publish a stream namespace and index table; define family-level keys, root counts/IDs, split ordering, label convention at zero and signed-permutation sampling. Specify the sharing of teacher, maps, rows and prefix across roots, behaviours, regimes and repetitions; define paired optimizer streams so different action paths cannot shift unrelated random draws. Freeze the invocation and deterministic manifest-generation recipe now. Manifests may be generated by the sealed implementation later, but that implementation must have no freedom to select them.

## MAJOR M1 — Threshold summaries and early termination are not fully defined

**Section and quote:** R1–R9, lines 209–215: “use attempt-1 tie convention verbatim”; “Between-family SD(later−never)”; “Verification lowers U”; “root SD”; “early termination < .5.”

**Problem:** Two repetitions yield several legitimate summaries, but the draft does not state whether family winners, harm, R4 gain and SD operate on repetition means, individual repetitions or a selected repetition. The SD denominator is explicit only for R6. R5 does not identify which verified regime is compared with never. “Early termination” lacks an operational definition, particularly when submitting the best visible candidate is allowed and every tail otherwise has a fixed eight-slot schedule. An implementer could materially change acceptance without changing a numerical threshold.

**Concrete pre-seal fix:** Include the exact formulas and denominators for all R1–R9 statistics, the ordering of family/repetition/behaviour aggregation, the inherited tie rule, and handling of invalid versus integrity-failed leaves. Define the R5 contrast and root-level reach/termination events. If importing the earlier tie convention, include its literal definition or an immutable, hashed specification in the seal package; do not infer it from the report's winner counts. Preserve thresholds and explain any interpretive change before D-ATT2.

## MAJOR M2 — The numerical distinctness rule can accept coordinate copies

**Section and quote:** Families and leakage audit, lines 168–171: distance uses teacher Euclidean distance and covariance Frobenius distance, “ignoring IDs/seeds/names.” Lines 182–185 acknowledge “Potentially symmetric laws” and ask whether “the signed-coordinate law and shared generator make this test too weak.”

**Problem:** The metric compares coefficients in their written coordinate order. A simultaneous signed coordinate permutation of teacher and covariance can represent an equivalent classification law under the declared neutral fields and candidate class, yet receive substantial raw distance. Thus the test does not exclude the very symmetry introduced by the deployment mechanism. Independent generation and no shared rows remain meaningful, but this distance alone cannot certify semantic task diversity. This criticism is a mathematical counterexample to the criterion, not a claim that a sampled pair has failed it.

**Concrete pre-seal fix:** For the intended task-equivalence scope, compare laws jointly under permitted coordinate symmetries. One explicit choice is the minimum over signed permutation matrices P of max(||w_i−P w_j||₂/2, ||Σ_i−P Σ_j Pᵀ||_F/max(||Σ_i||_F,||Σ_j||_F)). Keep the declared .10 threshold and no-redraw rule unless the author adopts a purpose-based amendment before outcomes. Specify exactly which symmetry group is appropriate given careless's feature restriction. Alternatively, disposition this as a deliberate identity-coordinate screen and narrow the diversity claim accordingly; it must not be called a symmetry-aware near-duplicate exclusion. Independent units and semantic diversity are separate properties. Freeze which histories are compared, the invariant-boilerplate removal list, empty/short-history handling, and the treatment of expected paired-prefix copies; those choices affect the second audit too.

## MINOR N1 — Authorized current-map output conflicts with the secrecy wording

**Section and quote:** Diagnostic mechanism, lines 63–64: output is “current feature-map correspondence.” Ground truth, lines 202–203: “ground-truth map never appear[s] ... [in] visible history.” Families section, lines 186–187 references a “descriptor allowlist” and says no “label/teacher/map truth” enters controller inputs.

**Problem:** Current correspondence is precisely the current deployment map and the returned executable decoder may encode it. If verifier output is part of recorded history, the absolute secrecy statement is false. Executor-visible information and controller-visible descriptors are different surfaces, but the allowlist is not specified here. The current wording could cause a genuine authorized probe result to be mistaken for leakage, or permit an unintended feature through an unspecified exception.

**Concrete pre-seal fix:** Enumerate executor inputs, verifier outputs, controller descriptors and host-only recorder fields. Explicitly permit current correspondence only after an audit, while withholding future map, seed, teacher and hidden data. Specify how history is filtered for H1/H2 and how executable decoder contents are treated. State that visible training labels are intentionally available; the protected label surface is hidden outcomes and other forbidden truth.
