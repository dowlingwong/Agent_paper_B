\section{Registered fault mechanisms and the separation requirement}
\label{app:faults}

\emph{Status.} This appendix is a retained registered design for the deferred
program; none of the fault families has been executed on a live agent, and the
completed measurement panels do not depend on it.  In the retained plan,
\cref{thm:ordinal} would make the design uninformative unless the positive
population can order baseline risk and verification advantage differently.  It
does not license creating that population by conditioning fault placement on the
risk score under test.  The machine-readable declaration is
\texttt{protocols/fault\_families.yaml}; it is frozen and approved under
\texttt{Contract A v1.3-rc}, the version the signed records carry
(\cref{app:protocol} states how that string relates to the local lineage).

F1-ORD records the two earlier exact-postcondition mechanisms: substituting an
altered solver heuristic and redirecting a trusted data or configuration
dependency to a stale manifest.  Their previous simulated Spearman correlation
of about $+0.32$ and $51\%$ advantage loss do not establish a near-null control.
W5 therefore retires the formal equivalence procedure and reports both mechanisms
only as historical MLGym-specific contrasts.  Reinstating a control would require
a new same-substrate mechanism, procedure, power analysis, and approval.

The authors saw the earlier F1 precursor result before
$\delta_{F1}=0.01$ was proposed, so no blindness or outcome-blindness claim is
made.  The candidate meant at most
one expected validated completion per one hundred selected checkpoints at every
registered capacity, but it is not an active tolerance because the formal
control was retired before any new equivalence run.

F2-FEHPO formalizes a natural semantic error: a feature pipeline appears weak
under a fixed, inherited, or mismatched downstream configuration and is judged
intrinsically poor, although it becomes valuable under a registered matched
configuration.  CoFEH calls the corresponding sequential-search failure
\emph{greedy myopia} and documents that feature engineering and hyperparameter
optimization can interact \citep{cofeh2026}.  This supports plausibility only;
it does not establish occurrence at agent checkpoints, identify our
intervention effect, validate an injector, or supply a completion-effect prior.
The exact task, feature candidate, and configuration pair are frozen in the
pair artifact (\texttt{protocols/f2\_fehpo\_pair.yaml}), instantiated on the
seeded group-disjoint pair-development subset after the production
checkpoint-restoration capability audit passed.

The injector-only record contains the assigned configuration pair and clean
counterfactual, but it is excluded from the agent, verifier, policy input, and
availability decision.  Verification may diagnose only the registered
pipeline--configuration mismatch.  Recovery applies only the registered
matched configuration and then invokes the same continuation horizon; it may
not become unconstrained hyperparameter search.

Within-checkpoint repetitions can reduce measurement noise but never increase
the number of roots or of independent templates.  The frozen F2-FEHPO report registers 500
E3 roots (maximum authorized 750) --- a count now contested, see
\cref{app:e3-estimator} --- the candidate repetition set $\{1,2,4,8\}$, and the
paired-seed rule.  $R=1$ is admissible but unpowered; only $\{2,4,8\}$ carry the
powered comparison.  After a qualifying disjoint pilot, the
mechanical R5-2B amendment selects the within-checkpoint repetition count
without consulting the E3 effect.  F1-ORD and F2-ADV remain historical contrasts.  If
F2-FEHPO is infeasible or underpowered, the
confirmatory claim is withdrawn or changed through a new scientific approval;
historical contrasts are never promoted.
