## From question to claim

Which control decisions improve a campaign's final configuration at the same budget, and by how much? In the
Sweep 1 replay, 3,274 of 4,999 proposals repeated a configuration already evaluated. That count motivates
testing whether skipping repeated proposals saves enough budget to improve the final configuration. D.3 pairs
arms on the same fresh campaign instances, each capped at 20 worker runs and 80 manager calls. Ten fresh seeds
measure the improvement from the starting to the final configuration, in units of the node's noise. A0R minus
A0 measures the gain from skipping repeated configurations and detectable no-ops. A2 minus A0R measures the
added gain from the noise-aware rule. A3 and A5 versus A2 measure what the learned forecasts add. The paired
estimates, intervals and declared tests determine which campaign gains the paper can claim. The preregistered
offline gate measures how those forecasts affect one-step decisions on logged proposals.
