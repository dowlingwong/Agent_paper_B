## Question, method, measured result and claim

Which decisions help an automated search improve its final configuration on the same budget?
In the sweep-1 replay, 3,274 of 4,999 proposals repeated a configuration already evaluated.
That count shows how often proposals repeated and motivates testing whether skipping them improves
the final result. D.3 compares controllers on the same fresh instances, with a budget of 20 worker
runs and a cap of 80 manager calls per campaign. Ten fresh audit seeds measure each final
configuration's improvement over its starting configuration. A0R versus A0 estimates the value of
skipping duplicates and detectable no-ops. A2 versus A0R estimates what the noise-aware one-step
rule adds with those filters in place. A3 and A5 versus A2 estimate what the learned forecasts
add. The campaign claims await paired estimates, 95% intervals and the declared Holm decisions.
The preregistered offline gate separately tests whether the forecasts improve one-step decisions
on logged proposals.
