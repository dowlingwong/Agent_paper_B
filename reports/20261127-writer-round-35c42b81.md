## Question, measurement and claim

all arms have the same caps of 20 worker runs and 80 manager calls? In the published Sweep 1 replay, 3,274 of
4,999 proposals repeated a configuration. That count motivates skipping duplicates. D.3 runs the arms on
matched new instances and measures each final configuration's gain over its start on ten fresh audit seeds,
in units of the node's reference standard deviation. A0R − A0 estimates what skipping duplicates and detectable
no-ops adds to the default loop. A2 − A0R estimates what the noise-aware decision rule adds with those skips
in place. A3 − A2 and A5 − A2 estimate what each learned forecast adds to that rule. Paired estimates, intervals
and the declared Holm decisions determine the supported campaign claims. The separate offline gate tests
one-step decisions on logged proposals. D.3b follows arms freeze and its own authorization under either
gate verdict.
