## Question, measurement and claim

Which decisions leave the search with a better final configuration under the same budget? In the Sweep 1 replay, 3,274 of 4,999 proposals repeated a configuration the search had already evaluated. D.3 tests whether skipping such proposals improves the final result. It pairs arms on fresh instances and starting configurations, caps each campaign at 20 worker runs and 80 manager calls, and measures improvement over the start on ten fresh audit seeds. A0R − A0 estimates the gain from skipping duplicate proposals and detectable no-ops. A2 − A0R estimates what the noise-aware index adds with the same rule layer. A3 and A5 versus A2 estimate what proposal-level forecasts add. The paired estimates, intervals and declared Holm decisions will determine which gains the paper can claim. The offline gate separately tests whether forecasts improve one-step decisions on logged proposals.
