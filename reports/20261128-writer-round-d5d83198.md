## Question, measurement and claim

Which control decisions improve the final configuration at the same search budget? In the first sweep, 3,274 of 4,999 replayed proposals repeated a configuration already evaluated. That count measures how often proposals repeat. D.3 tests the consequences by running the arms on the same fresh instances, with caps of 20 worker runs and 80 manager calls. Ten fresh seeds measure the final configuration's gain over its start, scaled by the node's noise. A0R versus A0 measures the effect of skipping duplicates and detectable no-ops. A2 versus A0R measures the incremental effect of the noise-aware rule with those skips in place. A3 and A5 versus A2 test the added value of learned forecasts. Their paired effect estimates, intervals and declared Holm decisions will determine the campaign claims. The offline gate separately tests one-step utility on logged proposals.
