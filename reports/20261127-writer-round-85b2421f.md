runs and 80 manager calls? Sweep 1 replay classified 3,274 of 4,999 proposals as
duplicates. This motivates testing whether skipping duplicates and detectable
no-ops improves the campaign. D.3 pairs fresh campaigns on the same instances and
measures the final configuration's gain over the start on ten new audit seeds,
scaled by each node's reference standard deviation. A0R − A0 measures the gain
from the skips under the default keep rule. A2 − A0R measures what the noise-aware
rule adds with skips in place. A3 − A2 and A5 − A2 measure what each learned
forecast adds. The paired estimates, intervals and signed within-family rules

decisions on logged proposals. D.3b runs after arms freeze and authorisation
