## Question, measurement and claim

Which decisions leave an automated search with a better final configuration at the same budget?
In the Sweep 1 replay, 3,274 of 4,999 proposals repeated a configuration the search had already
evaluated. That count establishes how common repeats were in the replay and motivates the campaign
test. D.3 runs each arm on the same starting configurations and instances, with a cap of 20 worker
runs and 80 manager calls. It remeasures the improvement of each final configuration over its start
on ten fresh seeds. A0R − A0 measures the effect of skipping repeats and detectable no-ops. A2 − A0R
measures what the noise-aware rule adds. A3 and A5 versus A2 measure what proposal-level forecasts
add. The campaign estimates, intervals and declared Holm decisions will determine which gains the
paper can claim. The offline gate tests improvement in one-step decisions on logged proposals and
has its own pending verdict. [Sweep 1 replay](https://github.com/dowlingwong/Agent_paper_B/blob/980c6666/docs/r1/reports/MAC_M3_DRYRUNS_20261104.md) ·
