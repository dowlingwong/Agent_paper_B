## Question, method and evidence

Which control decisions improve a campaign's final configuration under the same budget? In the Sweep 1 replay, 3,274 of 4,999 proposals repeated an evaluated configuration. That count shows how often the replay repeated work and motivates testing whether skipping repeats improves the final result. D.3 pairs arms on fresh campaign instances with 20 worker runs and a cap of 80 manager calls. Ten fresh seeds measure each final configuration's gain over its starting configuration, in units of the node's noise. A0R minus A0 estimates what skipping duplicates and detectable no-ops adds. A2 minus A0R estimates what the noise-aware rule adds when both arms have those skips. A3 and A5 versus A2 estimate what their learned forecasts add. The released paired estimates, intervals and declared tests will determine what the paper can claim about campaign gains. The offline gate measures how forecasts affect one-step decisions on logged proposals.
