## Question, measurement and claim

Which control decisions yield a better final configuration with the same budget? The Sweep 1 replay found that 3,274 of 4,999 proposals repeated an already evaluated configuration. That count measures duplication in the replay. D.3 shares starting configurations and campaign instances across arms, with 20 worker runs and at most 80 manager calls per campaign. Ten fresh audit seeds measure the final configuration's improvement over the start, in units of the node's noise scale. A0R minus A0 estimates the gain from skipping duplicates and detectable no-ops. A2 minus A0R estimates the added gain from the noise-aware index policy when both arms skip the same kinds of proposals. A3 and A5 compared with A2 estimate the gain from forecasting each proposal's expected increment. The released paired estimates, intervals and declared tests will determine what the paper can claim about those gains. The offline gate separately tests whether the forecasts improve one-step decisions on logged proposals.
