## Question, method and evidence

Which decisions help an automated search finish with a better configuration at the same budget? In sweep 1, 3,274 of 4,999 replayed proposals repeated a configuration the search had already tried. This count shows how often proposals repeat. To measure what skipping them buys, D.3 compares campaigns on the same fresh instances with up to 20 worker runs and 80 manager calls per campaign. The outcome is the final configuration's gain over its start, measured on ten fresh seeds and scaled by the task's noise. A0R versus A0 measures what the duplicate and detectable no-op filter adds. A2 versus A0R measures what the noise-aware rule adds once the filter is in place. A3 and A5 versus A2 measure what learned forecasts add to that rule. The paired estimates, intervals and declared Holm decisions will support the campaign claims. The offline gate separately asks whether the forecasts improve individual decisions on logged proposals.
