## Question, measurement and claim

Which control decisions improve a campaign at the same caps of 20 worker runs and 80 manager calls,
and by how much? In the published Sweep 1 replay, 3,274 of 4,999 proposals repeated a configuration.
That count motivates the rule layer. D.3 runs the arms on matched fresh campaign instances and measures
the final configuration's gain over the starting configuration using ten fresh audit seeds. It reports
the gain in units of the node's reference standard deviation. A0R − A0 estimates what the rule layer
adds to default keep. A2 − A0R estimates what the noise-aware decision rule adds with that layer
already in place. A3 − A2 and A5 − A2 estimate what each learned forecast adds to that rule.
The paired effects, intervals and declared Holm decisions determine which campaign claims are supported.
The separate, unread offline gate tests one-step decisions on logged proposals. D.3b follows arms
freeze and its own authorization under either gate verdict.
