## Question, method and evidence

Which decisions help an automated search finish with a better configuration on the same budget?
In the published sweep-1 replay, 3,274 of 4,999 proposals repeated a configuration already tested.
That count measures how often proposals repeat and motivates testing a duplicate filter. D.3
runs each controller on matched instances with up to 20 worker runs and 80 manager calls per campaign.
Ten fresh seeds assess the gain from the starting to the final configuration. A0R minus A0 measures
what skipping duplicates and detectable no-ops buys. A2 minus A0R measures what noise-aware decisions
add with the filters in place. A3 and A5 versus A2 measure what learned forecasts add. The paired
estimates, intervals and declared Holm decisions will determine which gains the paper can claim.
The preregistered offline gate addresses a separate question about one-step decisions on logged proposals.
