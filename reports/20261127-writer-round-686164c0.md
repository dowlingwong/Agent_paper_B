## Question, method, measurement and claim

At the same cap of 20 worker runs and 80 manager calls, how much does each control decision improve the
final configuration? The published Sweep 1 replay found 3,274 repeated configurations among 4,999 proposals.
That measured rate motivates a duplication check. D.3 runs each arm on the same fresh campaign instances
and measures the final configuration's gain over the start using ten fresh audit seeds, in units of each
node's reference standard deviation. A0R − A0 estimates what skipping duplicates and detectable no-ops
adds under the default keep rule. A2 − A0R estimates what the noise-aware one-step rule adds after those
skips. A3 − A2 and A5 − A2 estimate what each learned forecast adds. The paired estimates, intervals
and within-family Holm decisions determine which gains the paper can claim. The offline gate separately
measures whether a learned forecast improves one-step decisions on logged proposals. D.3b follows arms
freeze and its own authorization under either gate verdict.
