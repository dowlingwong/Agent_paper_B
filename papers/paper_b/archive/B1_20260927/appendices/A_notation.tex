\paragraph{Bellman reading.}
Let $\tau$ be the sampled checkpoint and $X$ the augmented state there, and let
$V^\rho_\tau$, $Q^\rho_\tau$ and $A^\rho_\tau=Q^\rho_\tau-V^\rho_\tau$ be the
state-value, action-value and advantage functions of the baseline controller
that selects $g_0$ at every checkpoint, so that $Q^\rho_\tau(X,g)$ is the value
of one deviation to $g$ at $\tau$ followed by $\rho$.  The arguments are $X$ and
not $H$ deliberately: the stratum $J$, the remaining budget, and the remaining
horizon all enter the transition kernel, and a value written on the recorded
history alone would have to be re-indexed by $j$ to be conditioned on $J=j$.
\Cref{tab:bellman-dictionary} records how the objects of \cref{tab:notation}
read in those terms.  It is a dictionary, not a method; \cref{sec:b-theory} says
what it is used for, and \cref{sec:b-data} --- not this table --- is what
identifies anything.
