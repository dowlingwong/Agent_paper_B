\subsection{Control errors of the default loop}
\label{sec:results-c1}

The logged sweeps show what the default loop gets wrong, scored against the fresh-seed audits. This supporting evidence says where the budget goes; the closed loop says what control recovers. Pooled over nodes, \COneFalseKeepSOne{} of the keeps in sweep~1 and \COneFalseKeepSTwo{} in sweep~2 had an audited increment that was not positive. The recorded gain of the incumbent exceeded its audited gain by \COneRecordBiasSOne{} and \COneRecordBiasSTwo{} $\sigma_{\mathrm{node}}$. Runs on duplicates, no-ops and invalid proposals took \COneWastedSOne{} and \COneWastedSTwo{} of the runs. Per-node rates, the missed-keep rate, definitions and a positive control of the audit are in Appendix~\ref{app:c1}. The two sweeps differ in the proposer and in its serving stack.
