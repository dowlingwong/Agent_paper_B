\section{Problem Formulation}
We formulate governed automated research as noise-aware dynamic algorithm configuration (DAC) \citep{biedenkapp2020dac,adriaensen2022dac}. At decision step $n$, a proposer offers a bounded edit to the current incumbent on an instance, or node. The configuration $\theta_n=(c_n,t_n,r_n)$ specifies a screening threshold $c_n$, a keep threshold $t_n$, and a verification budget $r_n$. These controls determine whether the candidate is executed, whether an evaluated candidate is kept, and how much additional verification is allocated, subject to the declared run and model-call budgets.

Execution produces a noisy comparison of the candidate with its incumbent. A skipped proposal does not reveal that comparison; a kept candidate changes the incumbent for subsequent steps. The controller may use the proposal, node descriptors, observed history and remaining budgets, but it cannot condition a decision on an outcome that has not been observed. Repeated proposals and no-ops are recorded explicitly, because their status can depend on which earlier candidates were evaluated or retained.

The target is improvement of the final incumbent, evaluated on held-out instances with fresh audit seeds and matched worker and model-call budgets.
