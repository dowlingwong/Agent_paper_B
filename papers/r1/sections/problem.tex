\section{Problem Formulation}
A governed loop proposes a bounded edit to an incumbent and observes an evaluation if it executes that edit. The controller can screen a proposal by executing or skipping it, and after an execution it can decide whether to keep or discard the candidate; verification is a further declared action when available. The observations, history and budgets available to each decision must be specified.

The planned primary estimand is final incumbent improvement audited on fresh seeds on held-out instances, under matched worker and model-call budgets.
