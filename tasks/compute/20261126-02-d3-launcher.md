# compute/20261126-02 — D.3 runner: the launcher must not depend on HTCondor (changes cluster/20261126-02)

Under R1-HOST-FREE the D.3 proposer runs wherever a card is free (Ada first; the pool when it has GPUs again). When
you build G1 from the accepted design, keep the campaign chunk logic in one script and make HTCondor one of two
launchers; the other starts the same script over SSH under a lease on the host. Two rules from the row:
all arms of one instance are served by one GPU model, and a campaign keeps its proposer on one GPU model from start
to end; the runner records host, GPU model and driver per chunk and refuses a chunk that would break either rule.
This is a design change: write it as a short revision of your design, get it re-reviewed, then code.
