AI-assisted independent review of commit `b0700226b0538a2d56aee212a4dfa5e59cbb1082`. **Verdict: not accepted.** The numerical proxy is reproducible, but two statements need correction or clarification before the author can use the proposal as the requested feasibility and scope review. ## Findings

1. **The same-data denominator is not shown to make the diagnostic optimistic.** Proposal line 9 says that the pilot mean-label denominator is “also an optimistic diagnostic.” `bootstrap_proxy.py` computes the base-rate RPS on the very labels used to construct that base rate, while Track P §5.4 specifies the mean of exam-train labels as the base-rate predictor. The in-sample denominator and its fixed treatment during resampling do not represent uncertainty from estimating a held-out training denominator; however, the cited sources and this calculation do not establish the direction of bias in the detectable skill difference or power. In particular, a smaller denominator scales both paired effects and their variability. Remove the unqualified “optimistic” characterization or substantiate exactly which quantity and comparison are optimistic. Explicitly flag that the denominator is held fixed in all bootstrap draws and that the output is conditional on it.

2. **State the required-versus-proposed scope with enough precision for the L5 decision.** The L5 brief asks which stages are essential for the claims, naming A5 and R-A0/R-A1, and what each optional stage contributes (`MAC_LOCAL_RUNBOOK.md`, L5). Proposal line 38 describes the latter two and asks the author which stages are essential, but does not explicitly connect A5 to the offline C3 gate and the learned-belief reference, nor distinguish the adopted E7 head-selection step from a hypothetical author-approved deferral. `RUNBOOK.md` Track P.4 and B.5 specify A5 for the offline table and RL gate; `TRACK_P_POST_TRAINING.md` §§5.7 and 7.1–7.2 specify E7/head-form comparison and the R-A arms. Add a compact claim-to-stage mapping, including the consequences of omitting each proposed ablation and E7. Keep the current careful qualification that these are possible scope changes for the author, not cuts already authorized, and retain the three-way-comparison and PLAN §9 constraints.
