>
> - Census evidence: `mlab_ogbn_arxiv` E4 took **21.8 s**, against a baseline of 5.651 s (record `u2_iso_mlab_ogbn_arxiv_…/runs/runs_edits/E4`). G.1b's timeout is 3 × 5.651 = **16.95 s**, and the census report states that value itself.
