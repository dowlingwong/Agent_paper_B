## Verdict: **REVISE**

The direction is right, and most of the cited facts check out. Paper B should make no multi-step-RL claim. The C3 gate and Track S should be separate branches. The comment's large Sequential Policy Track belongs in a separate paper. A logging contract is cheap insurance. But twelve findings need changes before the PLAN rows of SPR §9 are committed:

- Three factual errors would put wrong statements into the manuscript or the plan: the S0.6 sensitivity (F1), what π′ is (F2) and the "no train/deploy mismatch" claim about `budget_frac_left` (F3).
- One adopted author row, R1-TRACKS-FRAMING, would contradict the draft rows (F4).
- The vocabulary and the claim ladder are internally inconsistent and overstate the reduction theorem (F5, F6).
- Three of the five additions are under-specified or would not do what they claim. These are the budget DP (F7), the sidecar (F8) and the W5 checks (F9). The sidecar as routed adds work to a file that is contested on the critical path (F8).
- Two items a reviewer will ask about are missing: how the existing offline-RL (AWR-MLP) baseline is classified, and a history-updating Bayes-myopic reference in the C3 ladder (F11, F12).

None of these is BLOCKING. Each has a concrete fix, and none requires a new experiment.
