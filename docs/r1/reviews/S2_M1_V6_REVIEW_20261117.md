##### 3. Should-fix: the 24 GB floor is the cluster's reading (24,576 MiB), and the examples of admitted 24 GB cards are probably wrong

**What is wrong:**

**Suggested fix:**
- Say that 24,576 MiB is the cluster's reading of "≥ 24 GB" and that it may exclude nominal-24 GB cards. Add an author choice (keep 24 GiB, or use a nominal threshold such as 22,888 MiB = 24×10⁹ B) to note 6.
