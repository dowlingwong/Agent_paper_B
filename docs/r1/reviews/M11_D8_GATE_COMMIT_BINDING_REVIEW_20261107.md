1. **Committed symlink is not committed regular gate code.** `analysis_guard.py:119-130` uses `git show <head>:r1/c3/gate.py` without examining the tree entry mode. 2. **HEAD stability is checked before the full check has completed.** The second `rev-parse` at `analysis_guard.py:127` precedes worktree reads at lines 132 and 134 and decision parsing at line 137. A checkout change after the second `rev-parse` but before return is not detected. The report's phrase “checks HEAD did not change during the check” is therefore too broad.
