# E1b pilot headroom pre-check — 12 October 2026

**Label: `PILOT`. Not evidence (RUNBOOK §1, rule 3; RUNBOOK 0.4b).** The verdict below is a proposal for the author's E2 STOP. AI-assisted: run and written by an AI agent (Claude); reviewed by a fresh-session AI review (`docs/r1/reviews/BUILD_REVIEW_20261012.md`); no human review.

## What was run

**Nodes.** `digits_mlp` and `openml_ionosphere`, the two nodes E1 rated noisiest with material edits (`NOISE_FLOOR_20261012.md`).

**Instances and campaigns.** Six instances per node. Each instance seed sets the campaign's own training and split seeds. Each root campaign has a hard budget of W = 20 worker runs and uses the default controller.

**Proposer.** A seeded scripted random bounded-edit proposer (`r1/headroom/scripted_manager.py`), not the LLM: the VM that runs the pilot cannot reach Ollama. The Mac command at the end reruns the pilot with the pinned `qwen2.5-coder:7b`.

**Decision points.** E2 snapshots at trials 4, 8 and 12. That gives 18 decision points per node, 36 in all, from 12 instances.

**Branches.** At each point, `continue` and `verify` (r = 2, frozen response D̄ > 0), each repeated twice with the proposer at repetitions 1 and 2. Each branch runs on under the default rule until the 20-run budget is spent. Verify's four re-runs therefore replace later trials, so every branch costs exactly 12.0 runs on average.

**Outcome.** Y = mean val metric of the final incumbent minus that of the start configuration, over 6 common fresh joint seeds (5001–5006).

**Arms.** All L10 arms, cross-fitted leave-one-instance-out, with the instance bootstrap (2,000 draws).

**Runs.**
- **Main run:** `results/r1/pilot/E1b_20261012T161034Z/`. Harness `c686517`. Verify split policy `"fixed"`, as the author decided on 9 Oct. 144 leaves, 0 collection failures.
- **Sensitivity run:** `results/r1/pilot/E1b_vary_ionosphere_20261012/`. Harness `4e8fd69`. Verify split policy `"vary"`, `openml_ionosphere` only. Same roots, seeds and proposer, so the ρ branches are identical to the main run.
- **Invalid first run:** `results/r1/pilot/invalid/E1b_20261012T154045Z`. It was collected before the L7 resume defect was found and fixed (harness `f0dcd6a`). It is kept for provenance only and never analysed.

**Tables:** `HEADROOM_PILOT_20261012_tables.md` and `HEADROOM_PILOT_20261012_vary_tables.md`, generated by `python -m r1.headroom.e1b_analyze <run dir> --md <file>`.

## Headroom

Headroom = hindsight oracle minus the best simple baseline (here always ρ). Following the L14 review, it is reported as a bracket:
- the **naive oracle** is biased upward, so its upper confidence bound is a conservative ceiling;
- the **leaf-split oracle** is biased downward with R = 2.

| Run | Points / instances | ρ: mean Y (95% CI) | Naive-oracle headroom (95% CI) — ceiling | Leaf-split headroom (95% CI) | always-verify − ρ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `digits_mlp`, fixed | 18 / 6 | +0.0083 [+0.0064, +0.0101] | +0.0002 [+0.0000, +0.0003] | −0.0002 [−0.0013, +0.0007] | −0.0006 [−0.0013, +0.0000] |
| `openml_ionosphere`, fixed | 18 / 6 | +0.0189 [+0.0090, +0.0283] | +0.0004 [+0.0000, +0.0009] | −0.0046 [−0.0107, +0.0010] | −0.0014 [−0.0042, +0.0005] |
| both, fixed | 36 / 12 | +0.0136 [+0.0080, +0.0198] | +0.0003 [+0.0001, +0.0006] | −0.0024 [−0.0058, +0.0005] | −0.0010 [−0.0025, +0.0000] |
| `openml_ionosphere`, **vary** | 18 / 6 | +0.0189 [+0.0090, +0.0283] | +0.0010 [+0.0001, +0.0025] | −0.0047 [−0.0106, +0.0007] | −0.0007 [−0.0042, +0.0021] |

No tuned arm beats ρ by more than +0.0002 anywhere. The arms are the margin threshold, the KG index (pricing the frozen response under the campaign's policy), the ridge advantage and AWR-MLP; per-arm rows are in the tables files.

## Reading

1. **Question 2 (R1-GATE-FIRST) has a provisional answer.** For E2 verify-based control on these nodes, with this proposer and a 20-run budget, the headroom is small:
   - its conservative ceiling is +0.0006 (both nodes, `"fixed"`) and +0.0025 (ionosphere, `"vary"`);
   - ρ itself gains +0.008 to +0.019 over the same budget.

   Any practical target D18 at or above one E1 noise sd (≈ 0.008 on `digits_mlp`) fails. A target as low as 0.001 fails under `"fixed"` and is undecided under `"vary"`.
2. **Verifying at matched compute costs value on average** (always-verify − ρ < 0 in every row). This is what T2 Proposition 5 and the T3 simulation predicted: at a fixed run budget, four re-runs are worth less than four fresh proposals unless proposals are poor. The verify branch changed the outcome at 61–67 % of points, so the action is live. It just does not pay.
3. **`"fixed"` vs `"vary"` behaves as Proposition 3 predicts, within the noise.**
   - On ionosphere (σ_t ≪ σ_s), `"vary"` raises the ceiling from +0.0009 to +0.0025 and narrows the loss of always-verify from −0.0014 to −0.0007.
   - Neither change is resolved at this sample size.
4. **The C1 phenomenon is present; the remedy tested here is the wrong one.** E1 shows the default rule errs on about one material decision in six, and T3 shows the incumbent record drifting upward. But spending runs on verification does not recover value at this budget. The candidates T3 points to cost no runs:
   - a Bayes or Ladder keep threshold, which D4 currently forbids as a keep rule;
   - a posterior-mean incumbent;
   - E1 actions (skip, revise) that spend LLM calls, not runs.

## Deviations from RUNBOOK 0.4b (L14 review, finding 4)

- Only the E2 actions continue and verify were branched. Skip, revise, stop and demote were not; revise needs the LLM.
- The PACE-style gate is implemented (`r1/baselines/responses.py`) but not scored. It needs its own `verify:pace` action version under D4.
- The horizon is the rest of the 20-run budget, not a short h.
- The proposer is the scripted stand-in, so headroom here is headroom *for this proposal law*.
- With R = 2 the leaf-split oracle is noisy, so only the bracket is informative.
- `harness_heuristic` is degenerate at E2 (repeated_bad is always 0 here; it is an E1 skip rule).
- At 36 points from 12 instances, every interval is wide.

## Proposed for the E2 STOP (author decides)

- **Gate pre-check:** fail for E2 verify-based control on the current nodes and budget. Default: the fallback paper (C1 + C2 theory, PLAN §3), with this pilot and T3 as the negative-headroom evidence. Do not build L11.
- **Options before accepting the fallback:**
  1. Rerun E1b with the pinned LLM manager (command below). This changes the proposal law, the factor that most limits external validity.
  2. Allow a declared, run-free acceptance rule as an action version: a Bayes/Ladder threshold or a posterior-mean incumbent (D4 amendment). Then rerun E1b with it as a sibling.
  3. Branch E1 skip/revise under a binding LLM-call cap.
