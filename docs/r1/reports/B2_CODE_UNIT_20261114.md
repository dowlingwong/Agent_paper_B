## 3. B2 grid verifier (`r1/exam/b2_batch.py`)

It is built at the authority level of the B1 batch verifier (`b1_batch.verify_batch`, unchanged). It checks, fail closed:
- **Batch:** a completed EVIDENCE `b2` batch; configuration binding; registry snapshot; harness pin clean.
- **Finalists:** the B1 analysis digest and finalists; one candidate manifest per finalist, in registry order, and no other.
- **The author-bound grid:** the three nodes × seeds 9011…9011+n−1 (1 ≤ n ≤ 10, the author's cap), for the primary thinking-off mode and for the descriptive thinking-on rerun of the switchable finalists among the top three (the runner's own rule). Every root exists exactly once, with no extra root.
- **Candidate fields, as in B1:** Stage A pass and its provenance; `d28_pass` and `thinking_on_d28` recomputed from the verified primary and rerun roots; the anchor reference (`verify_anchor_reference`), as at launch.
- **GPU-h (the X.7 (a) tie-break):** each primary candidate's GPU-h is recomputed from the batch state's server intervals for (candidate, b2, off). They must be closed and not upper bounds (no `cost_is_upper_bound` or recovered interval for the candidate in b2). The result must equal the manifest's `gpu_hours`, with status `recorded_dedicated_intervals`. The ranking uses only these verified values; a missing, `None` or different value is a STOP. B1 has no GPU-h tie-break.
- **Each root,** with the same checks as the B1 verifier: receiving check, authorized HEAD, job parameters and authority, ledger diagnostics, budget, cache-false, observer and proposal journals, D28, replay, and paired audits from the cache. The audit seeds are 9201–9210.
- **Final-Y audits:**
  - exactly one per root, for its own (node, instance);
  - ordered seeds 9201–9210 (integers, not booleans);
  - ten finite numeric metrics, and `final_y` equal to their mean with an absolute tolerance of 1e-12 and no relative tolerance;
  - the candidate manifest's `final_audits` and `sensitivity_final_audits` are exactly those of its verified primary and rerun roots;
  - the primary pairs cover exactly the grid.

`_b2_summary_v2(..., grid=...)` then checks that the primary candidates equal the verified finalists, that each row
covers nodes × n, and that each row's GPU-h is the verified value. It ranks by B2 final audited Y, with ties going to lower GPU-h (rule `selection`). CLI:
`python -m r1.exam.analyze b2 --batch <batch> --config <cfg> --rule r1/exam/rules/x5_rule_v2.yaml --rule-sha256 <sha>`.

**Not implemented (as for B1):** the design's externally authenticated authority snapshot and its independent
receiving record (`B2_GRID_VERIFIER_DESIGN_20261104.md`, "Proposed authority anchor" and "Proposed independent
receiving commitment"). The independent review of the run and its evidence remains the process boundary. These are
for the author to decide.

B1 keeps its exact comparisons and calls (no `rel`).

The B1-only restriction of thinking-on to the sensitivity node does not apply to B2. Its top-3 rerun is descriptive
on every node, per x5_rule/v2.
