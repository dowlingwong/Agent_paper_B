# M10 HK6 unit interruption history — local pure candidate

**For independent review; not wired to a job, scheduler, author gate or runner.** HoreKa runbook §1/§3(5) says a unit interrupted by USR1 is archived, a later job reruns it whole from a fresh copy, and a unit cut twice is STOP. This helper inspects a caller-supplied complete ordered sequence of synthetic terminal attempts for one explicit unit ID; it reports `STOP_CUT_TWICE` after two interruptions, `COMPLETE` after a completed attempt, or `FRESH_ATTEMPT_POSSIBLE` if neither terminal condition is present. The latter is only a calculation, never execution, retry or submission authority. An unknown, nonterminal, failed, duplicated, skipped, wrong-unit or post-terminal sequence raises an error. It deliberately does not classify general infrastructure failure as a cut or auto-retry it.

The synthetic `unit_id`/`attempt`/`outcome` representation is a proposed adapter, not an existing reviewed persisted-record format. The caller must independently bind unit identity, reliably establish complete chronological history and provenance, and implement actual USR1 forwarding, open-unit archive, exclusive unit-complete publication, deadline and interruption cleanup. A fabricated or omitted prior history can mislead this pure function; it is not an authenticated history or scientific receipt. No K, budget, phase, author decision or new real switch is selected. Existing EVIDENCE refusal and off switch remain unchanged.
