## 2. Consequences

1. **C1 can no longer be presented as the first measurement of noise-driven false acceptance in autonomous experimentation.** Two workshop papers at NeurIPS 2026 measure it, one of them with the same meaningful-effect threshold. C1 stays, as supporting evidence under governance: fresh-seed audited ground truth on the T0 nodes, two proposers, duplicates and inert edits counted as wasted runs. Both workshop papers are cited as concurrent evidence of the phenomenon.
2. **The gate family is crowded.** PACE (part 1) and SAGE (part 2) were known; SGM and its descendants and the evidence-governed framework are added here. All of them control a false-accept probability and decide after the candidate has been evaluated. None prices a run, screens before execution, or learns. The paper's stated differences (R1-C1-POSITION): a rule that maximises audited gain per run under a declared price; screening before a run is spent, from a per-proposal forecast; theory for a ledger-dependent proposer; a learned forecast against a theory-derived index policy at matched compute.
3. **Baselines.** An anytime-valid gate arm was already required (part 1, item 4). It is a reference arm in the T0 closed loop D.3a (R1-D3-MIN), charged for its replicate runs, next to the fixed-r replication + test baseline of R1-BX-D7.
4. **Decision-focused training.** Calibration Is Not Control is the closest precedent for making decision utility, not calibrated risk, the target of the learned object (abstract level; read the full text before the manuscript says how it trains). The paper claims only the specific combination: a forecast of an audited increment distribution feeding a closed-form keep/skip rule under a run price.
5. **Endogenous proposer.** arXiv 2607.00871 names the endogenous proposal process as unproved for its gate. This supports presenting T4's results for a ledger-dependent proposer as a C2 contribution; read that paper in full before citing it.
6. **To read in full before citing specifics:** every row above (the six "Snippet" rows first). arXiv 2610.15738 ("Visible but Not Avoided") had its main text read on 19 Oct through a mirror, without appendices (`NOVELTY_FULLTEXT_20261019.md`); its appendices are still unread.
