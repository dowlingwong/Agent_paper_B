## Built-in decisions (do not reopen)

- **Proposed controller:** a cross-fitted continuation-value timing heuristic.
  - Checkpoint-1 score: `Â₁(H₁) − V̂_wait(H₁)`.
  - Continuation target: `reached₂ · d̂₂^(−k)(H₂) · (U^later − U^never)`.
  - Folds are by template family, and all descendants and repetitions stay together.
  - It is never described as optimal.
- **Primary comparators:** repeated-myopic, and a **context-dependent
  BAVAR-inspired VOI scheduler** (BAVAR-style signals, a tuned hard reserve, the
  same information and tuning budget, deviations documented). The **constant
  reserve is an ablation only.**
- **One shared two-phase hard-budget allocator** for every controller:
  `B = floor(.25·N)`, at most one verification per root, controller-specific
  cutoffs and reservation calibrated under the same tuning budget.
- **N1 lives in evaluation only.** Controllers never consume p. The population
  tax is `2·m·p_path`, where m is the allocator's displacement constant.
- Everything else stays as in RUNBOOK §12: U primary, q = .25, gain target
  .02, harm tolerance .02, and the p grid {0, .005, .01, .02, .05, .10} with working
  value .02.
