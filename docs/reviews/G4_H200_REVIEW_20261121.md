> ## 2. Findings
>
> ### Blocking
>
> **B1. The R1-BX-D14 recompute does not use measured throughput and does not model G.5 as defined**
> (`sweep2/g4/g4_lib.py:434-470`, `sweep2/g4/g4_dates_params.json:19-20`, `G4_PILOT_PLAN.md:134-160`).
>
> PLAN §10 asks for these dates to be recomputed "from measured throughput after the T1 pilot" (R1-BX-D14 at
> `PLAN.md:758`; conditions (iii) at `PLAN.md:741`). The recompute is a stated deliverable of this unit, and the G.5
> draft makes it G.5's P3 and P7 projection. Six problems:
>
> 1. **Run time.** Campaign time is rebuilt from the **census run times** (Ada, R1-ADA-CENSUS) as
>    `proposals × (llm_s + run_s × executed_share)`. The H200 measurements the pilot collects are ignored:
>    `seconds.run_median`, `seconds.campaign_wall_total` and `throughput_proposals_per_hour`. So are the per-campaign
>    baseline, reruns and the 3× timeout cost.
> 2. **Proposal count.** The proposal count is inverted as `ceil(150 / executed_share)`, which gives 250 per node
>    pre-pilot and 500 at a share of 0.3. G.5 is fixed at **12 instances × W = 20 = 240 proposals per node** (the
>    R1-BX-D8-V3-B author addendum at `PLAN.md:1234`; G.5 draft §2). Its audited count is
>    `240 × ok share`, not a constant 150.
> 3. **Audit count.** The audits are counted at the **executed** share, which includes divergent runs. G.5 audits only
>    proposals with an `ok` outcome, plus the 12 baselines × K (G.5 draft §2, §7). As a result the G.4 and G.5
>    pre-pilot numbers already disagree (86.1/121.0 against 87.5/123.8).
> 4. **Default K.** The committed default is `audit_seeds_K: 6`. R1-BX-D3 (`PLAN.md:747`) says K = 10 applies
>    everywhere until the K-check PASS row exists. The default output is therefore the optimistic case.
> 5. **Missing LLM median.** `float(s["seconds"]["llm_median"] or 0)` (`g4_lib.py:444`) silently uses 0 s when a node
>    has no LLM timing; the probe confirmed it. `ran_of_valid or 0` is handled the same way.
> 6. **Summaries not checked.** Nothing checks that the summaries come from complete, unstopped pilots: `stops`,
>    `pilot_stop` and `proposals == 60` are not read. Nothing checks that they are the three pilot nodes (duplicates
>    overwrite), or that they come from sealed outputs (see M3).
>
> **Fix:**
> - Per pilot node, take the measured H200 campaign seconds per proposal: `campaign_wall_total / proposals`, or
>   `llm_median + run_median × executed share + timeout and rerun overhead`. Use census run times only for non-pilot
>   nodes, scaled by the pilot's measured H200/census run-time ratio, and state that scaling.
> - Model G.5 exactly: `12 × (baseline + 20 proposals)` campaign steps, and audits of
>   `(240 × ok_of_proposals + 12) × K × run_s`.
> - Always emit **both** K = 10 and K = 6, with K = 10 as the binding one until the K-check row exists.
> - Refuse (STOP) a summary that:
>   - has a `pilot_stop` or a non-empty `stops`;
>   - has `proposals` ≠ 60;
>   - has a null LLM or run median;
>   - names a node twice or outside the pilot.
> - Report the slack per milestone against the D14 targets, and the G.5 total against the G.5 `cap_gpu_hours`.
> - Make the test assert the G.5-shaped formula and the refusals.
