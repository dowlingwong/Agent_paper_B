## Consequences for a full suite

The deny-only rule cannot presently be adopted as a transparent preservation mechanism. It may refuse a move to an absent name, which is also how authorized retirement to `_to_delete/` would normally be implemented; the exact writable-path behavior remains unverified. It does not establish prevention of truncate or overwrite. In pinned harness `a3fedbb`, `src/autoresearch/control_plane/campaign.py:173-183` attempts to unlink a pending guard and, on failure, falls back to `guard.write_text("")`. Thus a denial can lead to a destructive truncation while the trial proceeds; the behavior is not prevented merely by denying unlink.
