2. CHOICES THE SPEC LEAVES OPEN (please confirm)
 1. Members are anchored at m* (g0 = g*, h0 = h*), so tuning is two-stage: statics, then members. The `select`
    CLI reports the next stage, or the (policy, dev instance) pairs the collector must run.
 2. Centring is per epoch: r1_bar and tau_bar over E1 decisions, r2_bar over E2 decisions of m*.
 3. Overall B with mixed nodes: overall B if >= 1 B-eligible node meets every B condition and there is no Holm
    rejection; each node is worded separately.
 4. Tuning procedures (analyze.tune_members):
    - argmax(n): every member on dev instances 0..n-1;
    - halving(budget): ceil(log2 M) rounds with an equal budget per round; survivors (top half) extend to more
      paired instances; ties go to the smaller |parameters|;
    - channel_first(n1, n2): the middle size of each channel on n1 instances picks the channel, then its sizes
      on n2 instances pick the magnitude.
    The static stage is always argmax over 15 configurations x 20 instances (300 campaigns; +60 on extension).
 5. Flip rate: over pi*'s eligible dev decisions on the static dev instances; reported overall, E1 and E2;
    the >= 10 % B condition uses the overall rate.
 6. "Realised gain of the selected member" is estimated by the mean over replicates of the fresh S3e mean D
    (unbiased for the expected realised gain of the selected pair).

3. SIMULATOR FIXES MADE DURING THE STUDY
 - In run_s3e, pi* and m* on the same fresh instance shared one noise stream, which made D unrealistically
   precise. Noise is now seeded per policy; the exact placebo (same policy, same repetition) stays identical.
 - The revision-6 P0 "diffuse" variant (within-campaign size sd = 0.3 + strength) overflowed at large strength.
   It is replaced (section 4).
 - Calibration bisected at n = 600, whose estimate is off by about 15 % of delta_Y, and then refined at 1e4
   inside that narrow bracket. It now scans at 600 for the bracket and bisects at n_oracle (1e4).

4. WORLDS (rev 7)
 P0 budget. Two rev-7 attempts with pure across-campaign heterogeneity (an instance-level size multiplier; an
   instance-level rule-skip share) gave <= 0.2 delta_Y even for the unrestricted schedule. A threshold coupled
   only to runs remaining cannot tell a busy campaign from a quiet one early on. The current design: proposal
   quality drifts over the campaign (theta mean + strength f(t), unknown to A2-U's prior) at a binding W.
   diffuse: f = 1 - 2t/T; concentrated: f = 1 for t <= 8, else 0.
 P1 information. Fillers edit 3 recurring keys; probes open fresh keys (low one-step value, precise result) and
   schedule 2 large, noisy follow-ups on the probe's key; strength = follow-up size.
   concentrated: p_probe 0.25; diffuse: p_probe 0.8.
 P2+ keep feedback. A keep shifts the theta mean of the next 6 / 10 proposals by + strength x r (diffuse) or
   + strength x 1{r > 0.7} (concentrated).
 P2- keep feedback. A keep lowers the theta mean of every later proposal, cumulatively, by strength x r
   (diffuse) or strength x 1{r > 0.7} (concentrated).

5.5 Findings and recommendation
 - No design meets every rev-7 criterion:
   - P0, P2- and P1-concentrated cannot be calibrated to delta_Y;
   - P1-diffuse power at delta_Y is 0.27-0.38 (LB <= 0.31) in every design;
   - B is nearly unreachable even under N0: P(UB95 < delta_Y) is 0.97, but the flip >= 10 % condition holds in
     only 5-13 % of N0 replicates, so P(B | N0) = 0.045-0.13. Centred single-channel members agree with m* on
     about 90-95 % of decisions. The flip gate (or its threshold) needs revisiting.
 - N0 false positives are fine in every design (2-3 %, upper CP bound <= 6.4 %), and the N0 oracle gain is
   <= 0 with g = 0 and centring.
 - Pareto frontier (power vs tuning campaigns, P2+ and P1-diffuse at about delta_Y): D1 (380) -> D2 (460) ->
   D4 (636). D3 and D5 are dominated by D2. Magnitude grids buy little: P(correct member) falls from 0.8-0.95
   (fixed) to 0.3-0.7 (grid), and the realised gain does not rise. Racing (D4) beats grid argmax (D3) on P2+,
   but needs about 640 campaigns (696 with the extension, above the cap).
 - Recommended if the coordinator accepts a reduced control set: D2 (fixed-4, argmax n = 40, 460 campaigns).
   P2+ power 0.74 [0.67, 0.80] diffuse and 0.77 [0.71, 0.83] concentrated, N0 2 %.
 - Smallest planted gain reaching power 0.6 at <= 640 campaigns: P2+ about 0.9-1.0 delta_Y (D1/D2, LB >= 0.6);
   P1-diffuse about 2 delta_Y for the point estimate (D2: 0.63 [0.56, 0.70]), and LB >= 0.6 needs more than 2.
